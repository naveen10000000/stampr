PDF to Word Converter

Extract the text from a PDF into an editable Word (.docx) document. This is a straightforward text extraction, so it’s best for text-based PDFs rather than complex, heavily designed layouts.

🔒 Nothing uploaded ⚡ Instant, on-device 🆓 Free forever

How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload the PDF file you want to convert.
  2. Click “Generate Word (.docx)”.
  3. Download the resulting .docx file and open it in Word or any compatible editor.

Supported Formats

Input: PDF. Output: Word document (.docx).

Limitations

  • This is primarily a text-extraction tool — it is not a full layout or design converter.
  • Complex formatting, multi-column layouts, embedded images, and tables may not be preserved accurately.
  • Best results come from simple, text-heavy PDFs such as letters, reports, or articles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my PDF’s formatting and images carry over to Word?

Not fully. This tool extracts text paragraph by paragraph; complex formatting, images, and tables generally won’t be preserved exactly as in the original PDF.

What kind of PDF works best?

Simple, text-based PDFs like letters, essays, or reports convert most cleanly. Scanned PDFs (which are actually images) won’t produce usable text unless they already contain a text layer.

Is the conversion done locally?

Yes — all text extraction happens in your browser using PDF.js; nothing is uploaded.
