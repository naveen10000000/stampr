Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stampr really free?

Yes. Every tool on Stampr is free to use, with no sign-up, no watermark, and no hidden limits imposed by us.

Do I need to install anything?

No — Stampr runs entirely in your web browser. There’s nothing to install.

Is my data safe?

Your files are processed locally on your device using JavaScript. They are never uploaded to a server, so they never leave your device.

Does Stampr work on mobile?

Yes, Stampr works on modern mobile browsers. Very large files may process more slowly on a phone, since all the work happens on-device.
