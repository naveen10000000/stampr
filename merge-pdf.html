Merge PDF Files Online

Combine two or more PDF files into a single PDF, right inside your browser. Add your files, reorder them if needed, and download the merged result — nothing is uploaded to a server.

🔒 Nothing uploaded ⚡ Instant, on-device 🆓 Free forever

How to Use This Tool

  1. Add two or more PDF files using the drop zone below.
  2. Use the ↑ / ↓ arrows to put the files in the order you want them merged.
  3. Click “Merge PDFs” and download the combined file.

Supported Formats

Input: PDF files. Output: a single merged PDF file.

Limitations

  • Password-protected PDFs may fail to merge; remove the password first.
  • Very large PDFs may take longer, since merging happens on your device rather than a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF merger really free?

Yes. Merging PDFs with Stampr is completely free, with no sign-up, watermark, or page limit imposed by us.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your PDFs are processed locally in your browser using JavaScript — they are never sent to a server.

Can I change the order before merging?

Yes — use the arrow buttons to change the order the files are combined in. Reordering individual pages within a single file isn’t supported yet.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works on modern mobile browsers, though very large files may be slower to process on a phone.
