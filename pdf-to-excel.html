PDF to Excel Converter

Pull table-like data out of a PDF into an Excel spreadsheet. Each line of text becomes a row, split into columns — ideal for simple statements, lists, and tables.

🔒 Nothing uploaded ⚡ Instant, on-device 🆓 Free forever

How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload the PDF containing the data or table you want to extract.
  2. Click “Generate Excel (.xlsx)”.
  3. Download the spreadsheet and open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any compatible app.

Supported Formats

Input: PDF. Output: Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx).

Limitations

  • Works best on PDFs with clean, simple tables — complex or merged-cell tables may not line up into columns correctly.
  • This is a text-position based extraction, not true table recognition, so results vary by PDF layout.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of PDFs work best?

PDFs with simple, clearly laid-out tables — like bank statements, price lists, or plain data tables — extract most reliably.

Will columns always line up correctly?

Not always. Since extraction is based on text position rather than true table detection, complex or irregular tables may need manual cleanup after export.

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No, extraction happens entirely in your browser.
