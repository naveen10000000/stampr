HEIC to JPG / PNG / PDF Converter

Convert .heic or .heif photos from an iPhone into JPG, PNG, or a combined PDF. Everything is processed on your device.

🔒 Nothing uploaded ⚡ Instant, on-device 🆓 Free forever

How to Use This Tool

  1. Add one or more .heic or .heif photos.
  2. Choose your output format: JPG, PNG, or a single combined PDF.
  3. Click “Convert” and download your file(s).

Supported Formats

Input: HEIC, HEIF. Output: JPG, PNG, or PDF.

Limitations

  • HEIC decoding support depends on your browser; if conversion fails, try a different browser or update your current one.
  • Converting many large photos at once can be slow, since everything runs on your device rather than a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won’t my HEIC file convert?

Some browsers have limited or no support for decoding HEIC images in JavaScript. If a conversion fails, try updating your browser or using a different one, such as the latest Chrome or Safari.

Can I convert multiple photos at once?

Yes — add multiple HEIC files. Choosing JPG or PNG gives you a zip file with all the converted images; choosing PDF combines them into one file.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No, conversion happens locally in your browser.
