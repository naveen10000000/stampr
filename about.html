About Stampr

Stampr is a free, browser-based toolkit for everyday PDF and image tasks: merging, compressing, converting between PDF/JPG/PNG, exporting to Word or Excel, and converting HEIC photos.

How it works

Every tool on Stampr runs entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript libraries such as PDF.js, pdf-lib, SheetJS, JSZip, docx, and heic2any. When you choose a file, it is read and processed on your own device — it is never uploaded to a server, because Stampr doesn’t have one for file processing.

Why it's free

Because all the processing happens on your device rather than on server infrastructure we’d have to pay for, there’s no ongoing cost tied to how many files people convert. That's what lets Stampr stay free to use, without accounts or subscriptions.

Honest limitations

Stampr's tools are built for everyday, practical use rather than perfect, pixel-identical conversions. PDF compression rasterizes pages, PDF-to-Word and PDF-to-Excel rely on text extraction rather than full layout recognition, and HEIC support depends on your browser. Each tool page lists its specific limitations so you know what to expect before you use it.
